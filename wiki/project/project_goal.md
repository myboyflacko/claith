# Project Goal

Claith is an agentic development environment for individual developers and small engineering teams that turns human-approved plans into implemented, verified, merged, and deployed code. Its runtime operates independently and continuously wherever it is hosted, with a separate UI serving as the control panel. The system prioritizes reliable, recoverable autonomy and minimal human intervention during execution, while humans retain ownership of product direction and planning. It will build on LangGraph and coding-agent SDKs such as Codex and Claude, with Rust considered where performance and reliability justify it.

## Decision Guidance

Use this goal to guide planning and implementation. Make material deviations explicit. Current requester instructions remain authoritative when they conflict with this direction.
